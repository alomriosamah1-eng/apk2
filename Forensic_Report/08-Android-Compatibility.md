## Findings

### A-1 MEDIUM (high conf): `android:allowBackup="true"`
- Backup of encrypted SQLite DB + encrypted app-private media enabled, but keystore-backed SecureStore keys are **excluded** from backup (`@xml/secure_store_backup_rules`). A cloud restore to a new device yields encrypted blobs without their keys → **permanent lock-out / data loss**. Not a leak, but a data-availability risk. Recommended `allowBackup=false` (also flagged in Recovery/06).
- **Impact:** data loss on device transfer/restore. **Severity:** 🟡.

### A-2 MEDIUM (medium conf): `enableOnBackInvokedCallback="false"` with targetSdk 36
- Opts app out of predictive-back on Android 15+ (targetSdk 35/36). Can degrade back-gesture behaviour; Play targetSDK deadline risk for API 35/36. `MainActivity.kt` customizes `invokeDefaultOnBackPressed` legacy branch.
- **Severity:** 🟡.

### A-3 MEDIUM (high conf): `versionCode` fixed at 1 / version mismatch
- `android/app/build.gradle:95-96` = `versionCode 1`, `versionName "1.0.0"` while app.json/package.json = 1.1.0. `android/` is gitignored + regenerated by prebuild → `versionCode` stays 1 on every CI build unless `android.versionCode` is set in app.json. Blocks any release after the first.
- **Severity:** 🟡 (release mgmt).

### A-4 LOW (high conf): dead storage flags
- `requestLegacyExternalStorage`, `READ/WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE`, `USE_FINGERPRINT` — ignored/deprecated at targetSdk 36. Harmless.

### A-5 INFO (high conf): deprecated `expo.edgeToEdgeEnabled`
- `gradle.properties:63-65` deprecated alongside new `edgeToEdgeEnabled=true` (line 47); removal in SDK 55.

### A-6 COMPATIBILITY POSITIVE (high conf)
- Scoped storage, SAF, FileProvider, partial photo access, granular media perms, camera-blocking, no FGS/background conflicts — all correct and modern. Nothing critical here blocks the reported feature bugs.

## Android 10–15 compatibility verdict
Compatible. No feature is blocked by an Android-version rule. The media "doesn't work" failures are **not** Android-compatibility issues; they are RC-1 (no `items` table) and RC-3 (wrong directory) plus permission over-gating (A-0/07-P).
